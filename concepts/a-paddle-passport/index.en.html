FIELD GUIDE: WHAT IS PICKLEBALL?

Pickleball is a racket sport played with a solid paddle and a light plastic ball with holes, on a court the size of a badminton doubles court. It’s mostly played as doubles, the serve is underhand, and the rallies are long. Imagine tennis, badminton and table tennis had a very friendly baby.

  • The court: badminton-sized

    6.10 × 13.41 m, with a 2.13 m kitchen each side of the net.

  • The paddle: solid and light

    No strings. Bigger than a table-tennis bat, smaller than a racket.

  • The ball: plastic, with holes

    Light and slow, so rallies last.

  • 11 2 v 2 · win by 2

    The game: mostly doubles, underhand serve, first to 11

BADISCHE KÜCHE, NOW WITH A NET.

The kitchen is the zone right next to the net, 2.13 m (7 ft) deep on each side. You can walk in whenever you like; you just can’t volley the ball while you’re in it.

Tap a zone on the court, or a rule, to see where it applies.

4Games go to 11, win by 2. Only the serving side scores.

Badische Küche: no volleys in here

Rule 3 · Kitchen: Don’t volley from the kitchen: the 2.13 m (7 ft) zone on each side of the net. Step in any time, but there the ball has to bounce first.

Coming from…?

  • Tennis: Smaller court, underhand serve, no big serve to learn.
  • Badminton: Same court size, lower net, and the ball bounces.
  • Table tennis: The same quick hands, but now you move around a court.

Why people get hooked

  • Easy to start. Most beginners are rallying in their first session.
  • Made for company. It’s mostly doubles on a small court, so you talk, laugh and swap partners.
  • Every age, one court. Students, parents and retirees play together.
  • Nothing to buy. Your first session is free and the paddles are on us.
  • Your pace. The ball is slow and the court is small, so gentle or fast is up to you.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Still wondering about something? Write to the club.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. First Serve sessions are made for people who have never held a paddle.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. We lend paddles and balls from our Paddle Library. Just bring indoor shoes with non-marking soles.

What does it cost?

Your first session is free. After that, guests pay €[__] per session [up to 2 visits, TBC]. Members play regular sessions [at no extra cost, TBC].

What if I’m the worst one there?

You won’t be, and nobody minds. Beginners play with beginners first, and hosts pair people sensibly.

Which languages are spoken?

Sessions run in German, and most hosts also speak English. Hosts who speak French are marked FR on the schedule.

Can I bring a friend or my kids?

Please bring a friend. Add them when you book, so we bring enough paddles. Children from [age TBC] are welcome at family sessions with a parent.

Am I insured at a free session?

[Being confirmed with the Badischer Sportbund Freiburg and ARAG. We’ll state the cover here before bookings open.]

Is pickleball only for older people?

No. Students, families, ex-tennis players and retirees play on the same court.

How do I join the club?

Fill in one form and sign a direct-debit mandate, or pay by bank transfer, for example from a Swiss account. We’ll confirm by email.

Can I join if I live in France or Switzerland?

Yes. Membership is open to everyone.
