1–0–2The game

0–0–2? You'll know in 60 seconds.

Pickleball is a racket sport played with a solid paddle and a light plastic ball with holes, on a court the size of a badminton doubles court. It's mostly played as doubles, the serve is underhand, and the rallies are long. Imagine tennis, badminton and table tennis had a very friendly baby.

The whole game in four lines

  1. Serve underhand, diagonally across the court.
  2. Let it bounce once on each side before anyone volleys. That's the two-bounce rule.
  3. Don't volley from the kitchen: the 2.13 m (7 ft) zone on each side of the net. Step in any time, but there the ball has to bounce first.
  4. Games go to 11, win by 2. Only the serving side scores.

Badische Küche, now with a net.

The kitchen is the zone next to the net. You can stand in it, but you can't volley from it: in there, the ball has to bounce first.

9–0–2Questions

Good questions.

Not answered here? Ask the club. We reply within 2 working days (target).

Do I need any experience?

None at all. First Serve sessions are made for people who have never held a paddle.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. We lend paddles and balls from our Paddle Library. Just bring indoor shoes with non-marking soles.

What does it cost?

Your first session is free. After that, guests pay €[__] per session [up to 2 visits, TBC]. Members play regular sessions [at no extra cost, TBC].

What if I'm the worst one there?

You won't be, and nobody minds. Beginners play with beginners first, and hosts pair people sensibly.

Which languages are spoken?

Sessions run in German, and most hosts also speak English. Hosts who speak French are marked FR on the schedule.

Can I bring a friend or my kids?

Please bring a friend. Add them when you book, so we bring enough paddles. Children from [age TBC] are welcome at family sessions with a parent.

Am I insured at a free session?

[Being confirmed with the Badischer Sportbund Freiburg and ARAG. We'll state the cover here before bookings open.]

Is pickleball only for older people?

No. Students, families, ex-tennis players and retirees play on the same court.

How do I join the club?

Fill in one form and sign a direct-debit mandate, or pay by bank transfer, for example from a Swiss account. We'll confirm by email.

Can I join if I live in France or Switzerland?

Yes. Membership is open to everyone.

About this concept

Concept C: Rally Mode. The page plays like one rally: the ball from the logo serves into the hero, hops from headline to headline, and teaches the game on a 60-second court. Each section label is a doubles score call.

Homepage order: Sport-first. The explainer comes second, because most people don't know the sport yet.

Fonts: Saira (headlines, score calls, dates) and Inter (everything you rely on: facts, prices, forms). Loaded from Google Fonts for this preview only; the live site will self-host them.

Photos: every photo on this page is an AI-generated sample, marked "AI sample" with its shot number. The people in them are not real club members. They show the kind of shot we want. The live site will use only real photos from club sessions, taken with consent. The founders' photo and the board portraits stay empty on purpose, because they must show the real people.

  • Everything in [brackets] or marked SAMPLE is a placeholder.
  • Forms are demos and send nothing.
  • Languages: German and English; the French version will follow.
  • A coach must sign off the rules copy before launch: the serve variants, the scoring call and the kitchen wording.
  • On phones the 60-second court opens with one tap, so the sessions stay within two screens.
