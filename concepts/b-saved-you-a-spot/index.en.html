02Your first evening

Your first evening, hour by hour.

This is First Serve, the free taster for people who have never played. Thursday's starts at 18:30, and it runs the same way every time.

  1. 18:30

    18:30 Someone says your name at the door.

    Grab a name tag and a paddle from the Paddle Library. The host asks “who's new?” and most hands go up.

  2. 18:40

    18:40 Four rules, one host, no lecture.

    Pickleball is a racket sport played with a solid paddle and a light plastic ball with holes, on a court the size of a badminton doubles court. It's mostly played as doubles, the serve is underhand, and the rallies are long. Imagine tennis, badminton and table tennis had a very friendly baby.

    Badische Küche, now with a net. The kitchen is the 2.13 m zone on each side of the net. You can stand in it whenever you like, but you can't hit the ball out of the air there: it has to bounce first. That stops anyone camping at the net and smashing, so rallies stay long and friendly.

    Badische Küche, now with a net Küche is German for kitchen: the 2.13 m zone by the net where you can't volley.
  3. 18:55

    18:55 Your first rally, sooner than you think.

    Hosts pair beginners together first. Most people are rallying within about 15 minutes.

    yes, really Easy to start: most beginners are rallying in their first session.
  4. 19:45

    19:45 Last point. High fives.

    Tap paddles at the net, swap partners once more, and ask the host what comes next.

  5. 20:00

    20:00 The table afterwards. Optional. Apfelschorle counts.

    Questions, next dates and an optional drink, alcohol-free too. Stay ten minutes or two hours.

What to bring

You bring

  • Indoor shoes with light, non-marking soles (essential)
  • Sports clothes
  • A water bottle
  • Optional: sports glasses

We bring

  • Paddles from the Paddle Library
  • Balls and nets
  • A host who explains everything

The shoe rule. Indoor shoes with non-marking soles only. Our hall partners love clean floors, and we love our hall partners.

Your first session is free. After that, guests pay €[__] per session.

[Insurance for taster sessions is being confirmed with the Badischer Sportbund Freiburg and ARAG. We'll state the cover here before bookings open.]

We sometimes take photos at sessions. Tell the host if you'd rather not be in them.

Why people stay

  • Easy to start. Most beginners are rallying in their first session.
  • Made for company. It's mostly doubles on a small court, so you talk, laugh and swap partners.
  • Every age, one court. Students, parents and retirees play together.
  • Nothing to buy. Your first session is free and the paddles are on us.
  • Your pace. The ball is slow and the court is small, so gentle or fast is up to you.

Your first 90 minutes

  1. 00:00 Welcome: name tag, loan paddle, “who's new?”
  2. 00:10 The basics with a host: grip, underhand serve, two bounces, the kitchen.
  3. 00:25 Play: easy doubles with rotating partners, beginners together first.
  4. 01:15 Wrap-up: questions, next dates, an optional drink (alcohol-free too).

09Questions

Questions people ask before their first evening.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. First Serve sessions are made for people who have never held a paddle.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. We lend paddles and balls from our Paddle Library. Just bring indoor shoes with non-marking soles.

What does it cost?

Your first session is free. After that, guests pay €[__] per session [up to 2 visits, TBC]. Members play regular sessions [at no extra cost, TBC].

What if I'm the worst one there?

You won't be, and nobody minds. Beginners play with beginners first, and hosts pair people sensibly.

Which languages are spoken?

Sessions run in German, and most hosts also speak English. Hosts who speak French are marked FR on the schedule.

Can I bring a friend or my kids?

Please bring a friend. Add them when you book, so we bring enough paddles. Children from [age TBC] are welcome at family sessions with a parent.

Am I insured at a free session?

[Being confirmed with the Badischer Sportbund Freiburg and ARAG. We'll state the cover here before bookings open.]

Is pickleball only for older people?

No. Students, families, ex-tennis players and retirees play on the same court.

How do I join the club?

Fill in one form and sign a direct-debit mandate, or pay by bank transfer, for example from a Swiss account. We'll confirm by email.

Can I join if I live in France or Switzerland?

Yes. Membership is open to everyone.
